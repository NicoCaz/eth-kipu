Why a Safe Multi-Sig?

Security is a core value in Web3. The Safe (previously Gnosis Safe) is an industry-standard multi-signature wallet used by DAOs, teams, and protocols to manage assets and execute governance actions safely. By setting one up, students gain hands-on experience with secure asset management, a critical skill for anyone looking to work with or build decentralized applications.

Together, these two tasks lay the groundwork for both personal credibility and secure development practices in the Ethereum ecosystem.

Deliverables

To complete this exam, students must submit:

GitHub Profile URL – Must include a personalized README.md file presenting who you are, your interests as a developer, and your goals in Web3.
Safe Multi-Sig Address – Deployed on any EVM-compatible testnet (e.g., Goerli or Sepolia), with at least two signers configured.
